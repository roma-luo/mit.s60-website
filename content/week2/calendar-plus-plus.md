# Calendar++ Concept Document

Sep 28, 2026 · @Roma

## Opening

**Calendar++ is an agent-driven calendar. It uses agents to make the user's different goals clear. Each agent is given one of the user's life goals, and the agents evaluate and debate those goals to reach the best balance they can.**

## Related Studies

1. **AI raises assisted performance and lowers unaided capability.** A PNAS randomized trial: students who had used GPT-4 scored 17% lower than those who never had, once it was taken away.
2. People treat their future self as a stranger (Hershfield), which pushes them toward short-term goals.
3. **The field's attention is drifting from "augmenting humans" to "machine autonomy."** A bibliometric study of 1,223 HCI papers: the 2025 wave of "cognitive sovereignty" research was overtaken in early 2026 by agent-autonomy research, and frictionless design was named as a commercial dogma that induces automation bias.

We need something that fights the discount curve without thinking for the person.

> Good cognitive augmentation should not just give answers. It makes invisible disagreements visible, then lets the person resolve them.

Put several agents inside one person's head and the diversity is real. A 2,500-person trial supports this: several AIs with different goals improved decisions and preserved agency more than a neutral AI did.

## Calendar++

**What the user sees is a calendar.**

At sign-up, the user writes their goals into the app: a long-term career goal, the short-term things they want to get done, health goals, and so on.

## How it works

One week in Calendar++, four steps in a loop:

**Me** (goals in your words) → **Weekly meeting** (agents speak, the clerk tallies, you sign) → **Calendar** (every block traceable to a goal) → **Today's list** (checks = progress) → back to next week's meeting: signed vs. done.

Three layers that never cross: **agents only make proposals (tied to goals)**, each written independently, and they debate to win a say over the user's time; allocation is computed by rules (code); the decision is signed by you (human).

## What it is not

Not an assistant (it doesn't answer or execute), not a to-do list (these are your own goals), not a coach (no reminders, no encouragement).
