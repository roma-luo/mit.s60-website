II. Wormhole

Emotionally, people are almost strangers to their future selves ten years from now. A famous finding in neuroimaging research shows that when people imagine their future selves, the brain regions activated are more similar to those activated when imagining others than when imagining themselves.

Data Sources: Your values and long-term goals, as well as foundational behavioral data.
Key Capability: You can ask it a question at any time, and it will respond in the voice of your future self. Its answers are not prophecies; rather, they involve re-weighting the values and long-term goals you have previously recorded.
