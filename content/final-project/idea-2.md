II. The Debater

An additional brain module that reinforces critical thinking.

Trigger Conditions: It monitors three types of signals: when you use high-certainty words (“obviously,” “definitely,” “impossible”); when you’re about to make an irreversible decision; and when your judgment conflicts with historical patterns stored in the hippocampus.
Generation Strategy: This “organ” takes on the role of a specific person whose opposing viewpoint you respect.
Essential Constraint: It must not hold a position of its own. It is a generator of the “strongest opposing argument,” not an advisor with a personal viewpoint. If it begins to show bias, you’ll have a second source of overconfident noise.
